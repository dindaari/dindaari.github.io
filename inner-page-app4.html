FFTalent — Free Fire Recruitment Platform 🎮

Thesis Project | UI/UX Design & Flutter Implementation & Backend

Project Overview

FFTalent is an app that helps Free Fire players find the right team and lets teams find the right players. Everything is tracked transparently. Players can follow their journey, and teams/admins get automatic rankings and notifications. Fun fact: The app can ping you if your favorite team is hiring! ⚡

Problems We Solved

  • Players often have trouble finding a suitable team
  • Selection process is unclear or not transparent
  • Admins spend too much time screening manually
  • Teams struggle to rank players objectively

Who Uses FFTalent?

There are three main roles: Player, Manager, and Admin. Each sees features made just for them.

🎯 Player Role
  • Get notifications about selection stages
  • Check available teams
  • View personal selection status
  • Chat with managers or admins
  • Manage your profile
👔 Manager Role
  • Notifications for player actions
  • Manage team & selection data
  • Chat with players/admins
  • See player rankings & performance scores
  • View predicted skill levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Professional
🛠️ Admin Role
  • Full system monitoring & control
  • Manage players, teams & selection results
  • Oversee the entire e-recruitment process

How It Works

  1. Player Registration: Submit personal data + screenshot of gameplay stats. Only apply to matching teams. Admins get pinged instantly! 📬
  2. Automated Screening: KNN algorithm predicts skill level (Beginner, Intermediate, Professional) & gives a performance score (0–100). Players see if they passed the screening. ⚡
  3. Gameplay Test: Players follow the test schedule via chat. Admins track attendance & results.
  4. Interview Stage: Online interviews for selected players. Notifications keep everyone updated. 💬
  5. Final Selection / Hired: Accepted players join teams & history is recorded. Players and admins get confirmations. 🎉
  6. Player Dashboard: Track status, ranking, and performance metrics in real-time. All notifications reflected here.

UX Solution 💡

Players always know where they stand thanks to clear flow, progress bars, and visual checklists. No need to bug the admin for updates!

UI Highlights

  • Progress bar showing selection stages
  • Checklist for each stage
  • Color-coded status for accepted/rejected
  • Ranking dashboard for admins

Admin & Backend Features 🛠️

  • Automated KNN candidate screening
  • Skill prediction: Beginner, Intermediate, Professional
  • Automatic ranking (0–100)
  • Filter, sort & monitor players easily
  • Backend: Laravel + Filament Admin Panel

Tools & Tech ⚙️

Figma (UI/UX), Flutter (Mobile App), Laravel + Filament Admin (Backend & Admin), KNN Algorithm (Automated Ranking)
