Simparta - Pekalongan

The Simparta Pekalongan application is a mobile-based tourism information system developed to provide integrated and easily accessible tourism information, including tourist destinations, accommodations, transportation, creative economy, news, and tourism agendas. With search features, interactive maps, and API-based data integration, this application supports efficient information delivery and is expected to enhance tourism promotion and increase tourist visits to Pekalongan City.

  • Project Date: 2024
  • Role: Mobile Developer
  • Tech Stack: Figma, Flutter
